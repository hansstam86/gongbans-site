What it is

Application

Small MG24 Matter-over-Thread module with five PWM outputs, sized for smart lights and plugs. Protocol: Thread (Matter over Thread, IEEE 802.15.4); Chip: EFR32MG24; CPU: Arm Cortex-M33, 78 MHz; Flash: 1536 KB; RAM: 192 KB; Antenna: onboard PCB (1.74 dBi); Size: 15.0 x 17.9 x 2.8 mm; Package: 2 rows of pins at 2 mm pitch, SMT or in-line; Interfaces: 1 UART, 5 PWM, 1 ADC; Typical applications: smart building, smart home appliances, sockets and lighting, industrial wireless control, baby monitors, network cameras. Family: TS24 series. Source: Tuya public developer documentation (official datasheet linked), read 16 Nov 2026.
