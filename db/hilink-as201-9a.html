What it is

Application

9-axis attitude sensor: accelerometer (+/-16 g), gyroscope (+/-2000 deg/s), magnetometer; angle accuracy X/Y 1 deg, Z 5 deg; quaternion output 0.1~20 Hz; UART TTL 4800~921600 bps, no Bluetooth; 5 V (4.5~5.5 V); -40~+85 C; 15.24x15.24x2 mm. Source: Hi-Link English product catalogue (brochure p.27).
