What it is

Application

15 W; VR = 2:1 input 12 V (9-18 V) / 24 V (18-36 V) / 48 V (36-72/75 V) / 110 V (70-150 V); UR = 4:1 input 24 V (9-36 V) / 48 V (18-75 V) / 110 V (40-160 V); A = dual ± output, B = single output; 3.3/5/9/12/15/24 V outputs; DIP 25.4x25.4x11 mm; efficiency up to 90%; input UVLO, short-circuit, over-current, over-voltage protection; isolated regulated; 1500 VDC isolation; -40 to +85 °C; CE/UL/EMC, RoHS. Variants: VRA_YMD-15WR3, VRB_YMD-15WR3, URA_YMD-15WR3, URB_YMD-15WR3. Source: Hi-Link English product catalogue (brochure p.57).
