What it is

Application

22W (5V/4500mA) or 24W (12V/2000mA, 24V/1000mA); input 90-264VAC (130-375VDC); efficiency 85-90%; ripple <=50-120mV; 70x45x22.6mm; 3000VAC input-output isolation; output short-circuit and overcurrent protection with auto-recovery; UL94-V0 plastic shell; CE and RoHS; -25 to +60 C. Variants: HLK-22M05Z, HLK-24M12Z, HLK-24M24Z. Source: Hi-Link English product catalogue (brochure p.31).
