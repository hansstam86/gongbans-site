What it is

Application

Software project: DSP + AM/FM tuner on the PX3 car platform. FM/AM switching, station scan and list, direct station select, on/off and volume; FM 65-108 MHz, AM long, medium and short wave; Android app, service, HAL and kernel driver over I2C. ATU codename Hero-LR-TEA6831; ATU level S (sub-system). Status in the Sep 2016 ATU catalogue: 90%; functions working, closing documentation being written. Project code P16-030. Design files are not public: ask WPI ATU.
