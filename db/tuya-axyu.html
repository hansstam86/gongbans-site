What it is

Application

Wi-Fi 6 module on an ECR6600 Andes D10-based SoC in the 21-pin U footprint; a non-Beken, non-Realtek alternative for CBU-style boards. Protocol: Wi-Fi 6 2.4 GHz + Bluetooth LE 5.1; Chip: ECR6600-TS2D; CPU: Andes D10, 240 MHz; Flash: 2 MB; RAM: 512 KB SRAM; Antenna: onboard PCB; Size: 15.8 x 20.3 x 2.6 mm; Package: 21 pins, 1.4/1.8 mm pitch; SMT or in-line; Interfaces: 13 GPIO, UART x1 + log UART, ADC x1, PWM x5, SPI; Typical applications: smart home appliances, sockets and lights. Family: AXY series. Source: Tuya public developer documentation (official datasheet linked), read 16 Nov 2026.
