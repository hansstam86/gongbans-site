What it is

Application

Sixteenth-brick; 48 V 2:1 input (36-75 V); 10/20/30/50 W; single 3.3/5/6/9/12/15/24/28 V outputs; isolation 1500 VDC; efficiency up to 90%; -N = negative logic; 33.02x22.86x11.4 mm (50 W); -40 to +105 °C; low no-load power; input undervoltage, output short-circuit, over-current and over-voltage protection; CE. Variants: VCB48xxSBO-10WR3, VCB48xxSBO-20WR3, VCB48xxSBO-30WR3, VCB48xxSBO-30WR3L, VCB48xxSBO-50WR3(-N). Source: Hi-Link English product catalogue (brochure p.68).
