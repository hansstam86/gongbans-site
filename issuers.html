Who publishes gongbans

The catalogue indexes 1,121 boards from 22 publishers — chip houses, distributors, module houses, maker brands and one open-hardware community. Below them, a directory of 65 Shenzhen-area solution houses and ODMs that design to order. Public company information only: names, category, website, key products.

How to read it: a publisher put design files or documentation online — click the board count to open its boards in the catalogue. A solution house is a company that designs and manufactures to order; most of them never publish a file, which is exactly why a directory is useful. Industrial SOM platforms from Kingbrother are on their own page.
