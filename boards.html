The gongban catalogue

Chinese reference designs — dev boards, SBCs and SOMs, modules, chip-house reference designs and solution boards — as published by chip houses, distributors, module houses and open-hardware communities, alongside Hans's hand-curated ATU gongban kits. Filter by category, search by chip or vendor, and follow each entry to the publisher's own design files.

How to read it: every card is one board. “Full Design Pack” entries carry schematic, PCB, Gerber and BOM downloads on this site; “Design files at source” entries link to the schematic or PCB files the publisher put online; “Catalog entry” means specs or enclosure files only. Looking for the industrial SOM platforms? See the Kingbrother IDH catalogue.
