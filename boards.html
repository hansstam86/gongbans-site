The gongban catalogue

1,122 Chinese reference designs — dev boards, SBCs and SOMs, modules, chip-house reference designs and solution boards — as published by 23 chip houses, distributors, module houses and open-hardware communities, alongside Hans's 16 hand-curated ATU gongban kits. Filter by category, issuer, files or openness; search by chip or product; follow each entry to the publisher's own design files.

How to read it: every card is one board. Full design pack entries carry schematic, PCB, Gerber and BOM downloads on this site; Design files at source entries link to what the publisher put online; Catalogue entry means specs or enclosure files only. Industrial SOM platforms from Kingbrother have their own page; the companies behind the boards are on the Issuers page.
