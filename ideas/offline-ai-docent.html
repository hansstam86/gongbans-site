Offline AI Docent

A tabletop voice kiosk for museums and visitor centres that answers questions about the exhibition from a curated local knowledge base — fully offline, no cloud, no visitor data.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Small and mid-size museums, visitor centres, company showrooms and libraries in the EU.

Visitors expect conversational help; cloud assistants raise GDPR and AI-Act questions for public institutions; edge LLM compute modules now run small models locally.

What you actually have to build

Curator-fed knowledge base (PDFs, labels), wake-word in several languages, strict retrieval-only answers with sources, kiosk housing, usage statistics without storing audio.
