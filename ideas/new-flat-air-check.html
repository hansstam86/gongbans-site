New-Flat Air Check

A handheld formaldehyde + VOC + CO2 meter that renters, buyers and parents use for a week after a renovation or new furniture, then return — sold to rental and real-estate channels rather than consumers.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Furniture and flooring retailers (loan units), real-estate agents, parents with nurseries; rental platforms.

Indoor-air awareness is rising and formaldehyde from flat-pack furniture and new floors is a recurring consumer-press topic; proper meters are either €20 toys or €500 lab instruments.

What's new

What you actually have to build

Calibration routine and sensor-ageing compensation, a 7-day 'move-in report' PDF, rugged rental housing with lanyard. Gap: electrochemical sensors drift — plan annual recalibration as a service.
