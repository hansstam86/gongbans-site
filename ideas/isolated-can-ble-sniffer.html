Isolated CAN-to-Bluetooth Service Dongle

A pocket dongle that plugs into the CAN bus of home batteries, PV inverters, heat pumps and industrial controllers, galvanically isolated, and streams frames to a phone app over Bluetooth — no laptop, no ground-loop risk.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Solar/battery installers, heat-pump service technicians, industrial maintenance staff. (Explicitly not for road vehicles.)

Home batteries and hybrid inverters talk CAN; installers diagnose them with laptops and non-isolated USB adapters. A ready CAN-to-BLE module plus isolated CAN transceivers makes a phone tool cheap.

What's new

What you actually have to build

App with frame log, filters, DBC-style decoding profiles for common battery/inverter protocols, export to CSV; read-only by default, transmit only in a guarded expert mode.
