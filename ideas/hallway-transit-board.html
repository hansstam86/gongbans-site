Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Urban commuters in NL/DE/CH/AT/Nordics; hotels and co-working lobbies as a B2B SKU.

Open transit APIs are excellent in these countries; glance-style e-paper devices have proven that people pay €130+ for them, yet no consumer transit board is sold in continental Europe.

What's new

What you actually have to build

Frame, firmware aggregating 10+ national transit APIs with 'pick your stop' onboarding, side tiles for weather and electricity price; a tiny proxy handles API keys and rate limits.
