Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Athletics, alpine-ski, football and dog-agility clubs across Europe; PE departments.

Incumbents cost €800–3,000; grassroots sports funding exists; cheap DIY designs circulate but no finished €199 product.

What you actually have to build

Outdoor tripod-mount gates, ±1 ms time-sync protocol, sunlight-immune modulated beam, results app.
