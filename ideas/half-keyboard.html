Half-Keyboard for One-Handed Typing

A compact one-handed keyboard with mirror-layer typing and programmable macros, built from an open one-handed keyboard design, for people with a temporary or permanent single-hand limitation.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Occupational therapists, rehabilitation clinics, workplace-inclusion budgets, people after stroke or injury in the EU.

The established one-handed keyboards cost €400–600 and are a decade old; open mechanical-keyboard hardware and firmware make a modern €149 product plausible.

Boards from the catalogue and the role each plays. OPEN marks boards that publish schematic/PCB files; SPEC marks off-the-shelf modules or dev kits with documentation only. 4 of 4 boards publish schematic/PCB files.

What's new

What you actually have to build

Mirror-layer firmware (hold space to type the other half), left and right versions, OT-tuned key force, wrist rest, training app.
