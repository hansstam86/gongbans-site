Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Amateur theatre federations (BDAT in Germany, Little Theatre Guild in the UK), school and university theatres, churches with productions, small AV hire companies.

Amateur and school stages run cues by whisper and WhatsApp; the professional cue-light systems start around $485 and go to $985 (research, Nov 2026); ESP-NOW and lit buttons make a reliable set buildable from catalogue parts.

What you actually have to build

A stage-manager master with four channels and four battery outstations; standby (flashing) and go (solid) with acknowledge; ESP-NOW with retries and a link-health light; no WiFi network, no app.
