Control-Theory Balance Kit

A self-balancing reaction-wheel triangle and a BLDC momentum-wheel module with open firmware, so engineering students tune a real PID/LQR loop on their desk instead of in simulation.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Universities and universities of applied sciences (control engineering, mechatronics), engineering bootcamps, serious makers.

Control-theory lab hardware costs €2,000+ per station; open ESP32 balancing designs are proven and visually compelling; departments buy class sets.
