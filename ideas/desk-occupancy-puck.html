Desk Occupancy Puck (Thread)

A battery puck under each hot desk that tells the booking system whether the desk is actually used — radar sees a person sitting still, there's no camera, and nothing identifies who sat there.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Corporate real-estate and facility teams, flexible-office operators, universities with study desks.

Hybrid work leaves offices half-empty and companies are cutting floor space based on real data; works councils (OR/Betriebsrat) block anything that can track individuals. Radar + Thread is anonymous and low-power.
