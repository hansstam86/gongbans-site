Workshop Air & Noise Sentinel

A wall unit for makerspaces, school workshops and small joineries that logs VOC, CO2, temperature and noise exposure and shows a traffic light — the documentation small workshops need for health-and-safety visits.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Makerspaces, school and vocational workshops, small joineries and print shops in the EU.

Occupational-safety rules require exposure documentation even for small workshops; industrial monitors cost €1,000+; VOC, CO2 and noise sensing are catalogue parts (particulate sensing is not — state the gap).

What's new

What you actually have to build

Exposure logging with shift summaries, traffic light visible across the room, relay output to start the extraction fan, PDF for the safety officer; PM sensor as a bought-in add-on.
