Tool Check Wand

A handheld UHF RFID wand that inventories a van's or a workshop's tagged tools in ten seconds and says what is missing before the van drives off, with a local list and no subscription; on-metal tag reliability to be proven in the field before any claim.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Trades companies with 3–30 vans (electricians, plumbers, HVAC, builders), workshop managers, tool-hire counters; the owner pays after the second lost or stolen tool.

Van tool theft is large and documented (UK police data: 44,514 incidents in 2023, about £98m stolen), the existing answers are either locked to one tool brand (Milwaukee One-Key) or enterprise-priced (Hilti ON!Track subscription, Zebra readers at $900–1,900+), and the catalogue has a UHF reader unit.

What's new

What you actually have to build

A wand with a UHF reader and antenna tuned for a van, a local tool list per van and per technician, a ten-second sweep that lists what is missing, a buzzer, and an optional end-of-day check-in; tags are standard UHF where possible and on-metal tags where needed; no cloud account.

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

Do 10 trades companies tag one van each with 50 tags, achieve a reliable sweep (95%+ of tagged tools read in three sweeps, measured), and buy the wand at €229 after a two-week trial? Pass = 10 purchases and the read-rate met on real vans.
