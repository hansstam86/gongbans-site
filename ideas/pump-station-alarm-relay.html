Pump-Station Alarm Relay (sub-GHz)

A two-box kit for installers: a transmitter wired to the high-level float of a sewage or drainage pump pit sends the alarm up to hundreds of metres to a plug-in buzzer in the house — no cable trench, no app.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Installers and suppliers of domestic sewage pump stations, septic and drainage systems in rural areas; homeowners with pump pits.

Pump-pit overflows are expensive and smelly; the alarm panel is often in the garden where nobody hears it. Encoder-equipped sub-GHz OOK transmitters and stand-alone receivers make it a no-firmware product.
