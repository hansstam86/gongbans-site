Cell Grader

A bench tool that measures capacity, internal resistance and self-discharge of salvaged 18650 / 21700 cells and prints a grade label, so repair cafés and pack rebuilders can reuse cells safely.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Repair cafés, e-waste recyclers, battery-pack rebuilders and DIY solar-storage builders across the EU.

The EU Battery Regulation pushes reuse and repair; millions of good cells land in recycling because nobody can grade them cheaply. Existing graders are either hobby-grade or industrial.
