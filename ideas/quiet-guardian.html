Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Adult children (45–65) of parents living alone in NL/DE/UK/Nordics; home-care organisations.

Ageing demographics outpace home-care capacity. Incumbents cost thousands or carry monthly fees; Europe has no €149, no-fee, privacy-first kit.

What's new

What you actually have to build

Pattern learning and the family UX (daily 'all normal', alert only on anomaly); 1-year battery nodes. Hardware is trivial; the moat is software and trust.

Certification path

RED + EN 18031, EMC/safety, CRA, GDPR DPIA (behavioural data; consent of the monitored person). Wellness wording only — no fall-detection or health claims.

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

Put a mock-up in 5 real homes for 3 weeks: do the children still open the daily 'all normal' message in week 3?
