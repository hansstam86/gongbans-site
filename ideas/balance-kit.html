Balance Kit

Eight clip-on flow/return pipe-temperature sensors plus a tablet app that shows every radiator's spread live, so an installer does a hydraulic balance in one visit instead of three.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

German heating installers and energy consultants; heat-pump installers across DE/AT/NL/UK.

German building law makes hydraulic balancing mandatory for many existing buildings by 2027 and a condition for subsidies and every heat-pump install; the service is priced at €400–1,500, mostly labour.

What's new

What you actually have to build

Spring-clamp enclosure for 10–22 mm pipe, 8-node charging case, app that walks through the balancing procedure and produces the subsidy-office PDF. Gap: ±0.5 °C coupling and calibration on shiny pipe.

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

Ten 20-minute calls with heating installers: would you pay €699, what do you use today? Fewer than 4/10 saying yes unprompted = kill or pivot to consultants.
