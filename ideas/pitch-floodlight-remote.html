Pitch Floodlight Remote (LoRa)

Coaches switch the training-pitch floodlights on and off from a key fob or phone, per field half, from up to a kilometre away, with automatic switch-off after training and a kWh log for the club treasurer.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Amateur football, hockey and rugby clubs; municipalities that own sports parks.

Floodlights are the biggest electricity bill at many clubs and are often left on; the switch cabinet is far from the clubhouse. LoRa reaches across the sports park without trenching cables.
