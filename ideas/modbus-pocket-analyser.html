Modbus Pocket Analyser

A handheld with keyboard and screen that plugs into any RS485 bus, auto-detects baud rate and slave IDs, decodes registers of common heat pumps, inverters and meters and logs to SD — no laptop needed.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

HVAC and solar service technicians, building-automation integrators, maintenance departments.

The installed base of Modbus devices in homes exploded with heat pumps and PV; commissioning still means a laptop on the boiler-room floor.
