Mould-Risk Monitor

A wall puck that measures room humidity and the actual surface temperature of the cold outer wall, computes the dew point and tells tenants when to ventilate — before mould appears.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

German and Dutch tenants and landlords (mould disputes), housing associations, building-energy consultants.

Energy-saving behaviour (less heating, less ventilation) is driving mould complaints; landlord-tenant disputes hinge on who ventilated. A device that logs the physics settles the argument.
