HaptiKnob — Haptic Setpoint Knob

A wall-mounted motorised knob with a round screen that feels like a thermostat — detents, end-stops, resistance — and controls your heat pump, lights or speakers through your local home-automation hub.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

The million-plus European home-automation households and heat-pump owners who dislike the manufacturer's UI; installers of premium smart homes.

Open-source smart knobs proved the desire; Chinese brands are launching knob controllers; nobody sells a finished wall product in Europe with a proper mount.

What's new

What you actually have to build

EU 60 mm wall-box mount, 24 V/USB-C power, haptic profiles per entity, MQTT/Matter bridging. Gap: motor noise/heat tuning — the open designs are desk toys, not wall devices.
