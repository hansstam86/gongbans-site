Classroom Geiger Kit

A Geiger counter kit with a clicker, a big count display and a datalogger for physics lessons on radioactivity — demonstrate background, distance and shielding with potassium salt and a banana.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Secondary-school physics departments, science museums, STEM clubs in the EU.

Radioactivity is in every physics curriculum but school counters are 30 years old; open Geiger designs exist and public interest in radiation measurement is high.
