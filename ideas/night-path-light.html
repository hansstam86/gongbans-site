Night Path Light

Plug-in floor-level light strips that softly light the way from bed to bathroom the moment someone sits up at night, and go dark once they're back — radar sees people, not pets on the floor.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Older people living independently, their families, care-housing fit-outs; also parents of toddlers.

Night-time trips to the toilet in the dark are a classic trip hazard; PIR night lights trigger too late (once you're already walking) and miss someone sitting on the bed edge. 24 GHz presence radar sees the sit-up.

What's new

What you actually have to build

Two radar zones (bed edge, doorway), warm 2200 K low-glare light at 5–10% that ramps; optional weekly 'night trips' count to family (wellness insight, opt-in).
