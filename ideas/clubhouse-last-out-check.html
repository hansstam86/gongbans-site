Clubhouse Last-Out Check

When the last volunteer locks up, one sensor set confirms the clubhouse is empty, doors are shut and the heating and lights are off — and sends the board a single 'all good' message, or exactly what was left on.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Amateur football, hockey, tennis and rowing clubs; scouting groups; village halls run by volunteers.

Volunteer-run clubhouses waste a lot of energy and suffer from doors left open; club boards don't want a security-company contract. Cheap Tuya-based plugs and sensors plus a multi-target radar cover it.
