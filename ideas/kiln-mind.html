A retrofit controller and logger for the home potter's small electric kiln: ramp-and-hold programmes, a phone notification when the firing is done, and a log of every firing, for individual hobbyists, not schools or studios.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Individual hobbyist potters with a small electric kiln at home (DE/UK/NL/Nordics), kiln repair technicians as installers; explicitly not schools, colleges or commercial studios, where insurance terms rule out non-listed controllers.

Hobby kilns are booming with the ceramics revival, the replacement controllers cost $388–699 and are sold through dealers, the catalogue has an isolated thermocouple input and a DIN controller ready, and the research confirmed the insurance problem that keeps this a hobbyist product.

What's new

What you actually have to build

A controller box with an isolated thermocouple input, programme editor on the phone over local WiFi, cone-table presets, a pilot output to the kiln's existing contactor fitted by a technician, over-temperature and stuck-relay watchdogs, and a log of every firing; sold with an installer note and a clear statement that it is not a listed controller for institutions.

Europe

Certification path

LVD / EN 60730-1 class for a mains-connected temperature controller, EMC, RED (WiFi), RoHS, WEEE, CRA; installer-fitted; the kiln contactor and wiring stay the installer's responsibility.

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

Do 10 individual hobbyist potters (not schools or studios) buy at €299 after seeing one logged firing on a kiln like theirs, and does a kiln repair technician agree to fit it? Pass = 10 orders and 1 installer in 8 weeks.
