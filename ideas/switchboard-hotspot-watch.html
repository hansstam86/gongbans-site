Switchboard Hot-Spot Watch

A thermal-array sensor mounted inside a distribution board that watches every breaker and busbar for hot spots and alerts long before a loose connection becomes an electrical fire.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Facility managers, electrical contractors, insurers and property owners of commercial buildings in the EU.

Electrical faults are a leading cause of building fires; thermographic inspection is done once a year by hand; low-resolution thermal arrays are cheap and enough to spot a breaker running hot.

What's new

What you actually have to build

Per-zone baselines and ΔT alarms, 24 V or auxiliary-supply power, DIN-clip mount, LoRaWAN or WiFi, monthly thermal report for the insurer.
