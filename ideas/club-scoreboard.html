Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Amateur sports clubs, school gyms, municipal sports halls across Europe.

Hall scoreboards cost €1,500–5,000 and are rarely replaced; clubs run on volunteers and grants; an open P3 matrix driver and a cheap wireless remote cover the hardware.

What's new

What you actually have to build

Sport templates (score, period, fouls, shot clock), ESP-NOW remote with 50 m range, wall or tripod mount, companion phone display for spectators; pairs with the Club Timing Gates.
