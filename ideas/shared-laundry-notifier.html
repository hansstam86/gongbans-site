Laundry Room Notifier

Clip-on current or vibration sensors per machine and an e-paper status board at the laundry-room door, with optional push to residents, so nobody carries a basket down to a busy room; no subscription.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Housing associations, student-housing operators and housing co-ops in CH/DE/Nordics where shared laundry rooms are standard; facility managers buy, residents use.

Washer-done detection is one of the most repeated Home Assistant projects, shared laundry rooms are a daily friction for millions of residents, and the professional booking systems are expensive and tied to card infrastructure.

What's new

What you actually have to build

Sensor nodes that read a machine's power draw or vibration and classify running / finished / free, a door-side e-paper board listing every machine, an optional resident web page or push, and a LoRa link for basements; installed without touching the machines' electrics; no per-room fee.

Europe

Certification path

RED via modules, EMC, EN 62368-1 (clip-on current sensor, no mains contact; plug-through variant needs the socket's own certification), RoHS, WEEE, CRA; Battery Regulation for battery nodes.

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

Do 3 housing associations or student-housing operators install a paid pilot in 5 laundry rooms at €299 per room, and do residents say in week 3 that they check the board before carrying the basket? Pass = 3 pilots and a majority yes.
