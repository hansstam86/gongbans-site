Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Deaf and hard-of-hearing people, their families, social-care budgets and hearing-aid shops in DE/UK/NL/Nordics.

Specialist alert systems cost €200–600 and are rarely reimbursed in full; on-device sound-pattern classification on an ESP32 is now routine.
