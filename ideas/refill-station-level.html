Refill-Station Level Radar

A clip-on radar for the bulk dispensers and canisters in zero-waste and refill shops that shows how full each container is and tells the owner what to reorder — through the lid, no contact with the liquid.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Refill and zero-waste shops, organic stores, bulk detergent/soap refill points in supermarkets.

EU packaging regulation (PPWR) pushes refill and reuse; refill shops run on thin margins and run out of stock unnoticed. Liquid-level radar modules are compact and measure without touching food or chemicals.

Europe

Certification path

RED for the 79 GHz radar (check the applicable harmonised standard for level-probing radar), EMC, CRA. Food contact: none (measures through the lid).

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

Does the radar read reliably through the HDPE/glass lids actually used in refill shops? Test 10 container types; if more than 3 need a lid hole, rethink.
