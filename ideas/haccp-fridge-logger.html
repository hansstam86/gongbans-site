Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

EU restaurants, bakeries, food trucks, care-home and school kitchens.

EU food-hygiene rules oblige temperature records; most small kitchens still write them by hand twice a day. Incumbents are either subscription-based or Bluetooth-only single-fridge loggers.

What's new

What you actually have to build

HACCP workflow firmware (limits per fridge, corrective-action prompts, signed PDF, 2-year local storage), IP54 magnetic sensor pod, probe-through-door option, ≥1-year battery inside steel fridges.
