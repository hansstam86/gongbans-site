Industrial ESP32 DIN Carrier

An open DIN-rail carrier board for ESP32 projects in real panels: 9–36 V input, isolated 5/3.3 V, isolated RS-485, digital isolators on the I/O — the 'make it survive a control cabinet' board makers and integrators keep re-inventing.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

System integrators, OEMs doing small runs, makers moving ESPHome/Arduino projects into control cabinets.

ESP32 is everywhere in light industrial automation but hobby boards die from spikes and ground loops. Wide-input isolated converters and isolated transceivers are now cheap modules.

What's new

What you actually have to build

One open-hardware PCB in a standard DIN case, ESPHome and Arduino board definitions, surge/ESD protection, screw terminals, documented isolation barrier.

Certification path

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

Will 300 integrators buy a board at €89 when cheaper industrial ESP32 boards exist? The pitch is documented isolation — check with 10 integrators whether that's worth the premium.
