Vibration Health Sticker

A magnet-mount battery sensor that watches vibration and temperature of pumps, fans and compressors and sends a weekly health trend over LoRaWAN — predictive maintenance for buildings that have no maintenance engineer.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Facility managers of schools, hospitals' technical services, property managers, HVAC service contractors.

Industrial condition monitoring costs €500+ per point and needs a platform; mid-size buildings have hundreds of motors and zero monitoring; LoRaWAN makes 5-year battery nodes feasible.
