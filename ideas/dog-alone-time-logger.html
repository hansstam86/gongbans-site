Dog Alone-Time Logger

A shelf sensor that tells you how your dog spends the day when you're out — how long it paced, rested at the door or barked — with radar and a sound-level meter instead of a camera filming your living room.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Dog owners returning to hybrid office work, dog trainers and behaviourists working on separation issues, landlords with pet clauses.

Post-pandemic dogs struggle with being alone; pet cameras are intrusive and need you to watch. Radar trajectory + loudness gives a summary without video.

Europe

Certification path

RED (radar + Wi-Fi), EMC, CRA. Pet radar accuracy on small dogs must be validated. App and push alerts need the Tuya cloud — disclose.

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

Can the radar separate a 10 kg dog's pacing from resting reliably? Test with 10 dogs of different sizes; then ask 20 dog trainers whether they'd recommend it.
