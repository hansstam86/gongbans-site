Quiet-Hours Guard for Rentals

A host-owned sensor for short-stay apartments that measures noise level and how many people are present — without recording audio or images — and warns guests first, the host second, before neighbours complain.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Short-stay hosts and management companies in Amsterdam, Barcelona, Lisbon, Berlin, Paris where nuisance rules and permits are strict.

Cities are revoking short-stay permits over noise complaints; existing monitors are subscription-heavy and count devices on Wi-Fi rather than people. Multi-target radar counts people without identifying them.

What's new

What you actually have to build

On-device dB(A) metering + people count; escalation: in-room light/voice nudge → host message; disclosure sticker and house-rules text included.

Europe

Certification path

RED (radar + LTE-M), EMC, CRA. GDPR: must be disclosed in the listing, no audio content captured; check local rules on monitoring inside dwellings.

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

Will 30 hosts pay €149 + monthly when incumbent noise monitors exist? Differentiator must be people-count + no-subscription option; test with a host forum survey and 10 pre-orders.
