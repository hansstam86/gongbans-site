Fitting-Room Free Light

A radar above each fitting room that shows a green/red light outside and tells staff how long cubicles are busy — because cameras in fitting rooms are illegal and curtains don't tell you anything.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Fashion retailers, outdoor/sports shops, department stores in the EU.

Fitting rooms are where conversion happens but nobody measures them; radar presence works through curtains and sees still people, with zero image data.

What you actually have to build

Battery or 12 V cubicle node, staff tablet/screen with occupancy and 'waiting too long' alerts, weekly utilisation report; retrofit in an evening.
