Sleep Rhythm Bedside Radar

A bedside e-paper clock with a 60 GHz radar that shows last night's in-bed time, restlessness and breathing-rhythm trend — contactless, no wearable, no camera, nothing uploaded unless you choose.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

People who won't wear a sleep tracker, older relatives, wellness-minded consumers in DACH/Nordics.

Sleep wearables have fatigue problems; contactless 60 GHz respiration-rate radar modules are now cheap; e-paper means no glowing screen in the bedroom.

What's new

What you actually have to build

Trends only (in bed, out of bed, calm vs restless, breathing-rate band relative to the user's own baseline); 'compare to last week' views; local storage with optional export. No scores pretending to be clinical.

Europe

Certification path

Not a medical device: wellness/safety alert only — wording, intended use and IFU must avoid diagnosis or monitoring claims, otherwise MDR (EU 2017/745) classification applies — never claim sleep apnoea or heart detection. RED (60 GHz), LVD/EMC, GDPR (health-adjacent data stays local by default).

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

Does the module's breathing-rate trend agree with a reference chest belt within ±2 breaths/min for 20 sleepers, through a duvet? If not, the product is a fancy clock.
