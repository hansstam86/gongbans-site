Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Sports clubs, parishes and municipal hall managers who pay the heating bill; electricians and heating installers who fit it. Sold on cost savings, not on compliance.

Clubs manually fight heated empty halls (German sports-federation guidance describes exactly this), energy prices made the bill visible, and the building-automation rules that exist are sized for far bigger buildings, so nothing forces a solution and nobody sells a small one.

What's new

What you actually have to build

A DIN box that reads the hall's booking calendar (CalDAV, Google, the club's booking tool), a presence sensor, a temperature sensor and a schedule, and drives the boiler enable or zone valve with pre-heat logic; a local web page and a monthly report of heating hours saved; no cloud.

Certification path

LVD / EN 60730-1 class for a mains-connected control device, EMC, RED (WiFi), RoHS, WEEE, CRA; installer-fitted; the boiler interface stays within the boiler maker's enable contact spec.

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

Do 5 clubs or parishes install a paid pilot at €349 and measure heating hours saved for one month? Pass = 5 pilots and a measured saving in at least 3.
