mmWave Radar Dev Carrier

An open carrier board with footprints for the popular 24 GHz and 60 GHz radar modules plus an ESP32-C6, ESPHome-ready out of the box — so makers and product teams prototype any radar idea in an afternoon.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Makers, Home Assistant community, product teams prototyping radar products (like many ideas on this list).

Radar modules are cheap but every project re-wires UART, power and level shifting; a common open carrier with tested configs is the missing piece — and a funnel for the GONGBANS radar catalogue.

What's new

What you actually have to build

One PCB with switchable 3.3/5 V radar supply, 3D-printable enclosures for ceiling/wall/desk, ESPHome YAML per module, Matter build option on the C6.
