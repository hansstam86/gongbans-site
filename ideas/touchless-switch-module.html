Touchless Switch Module

A wave-to-activate gesture module, based on a full open design pack, that dispenser, lamp and furniture makers drop into their products to add touch-free operation without engineering it themselves.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

European makers of soap and sanitiser dispensers, bathroom furniture, lamps and kitchen fittings; contract manufacturers.

Touch-free operation is now expected in hygiene-sensitive products; small manufacturers lack the sensor know-how; a complete reference design with schematic, PCB, Gerber and BOM is already in the catalogue.

What you actually have to build

A 25×25 mm module with a defined connector, relay/MOSFET output options, false-trigger filtering, EU EMC pre-test report and a design-in guide for OEMs.
