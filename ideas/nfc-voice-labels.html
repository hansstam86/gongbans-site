NFC Voice Labels

A pen-sized reader and a pack of washable NFC stickers: record a voice note onto any tin, folder or medicine box and hear it back with a tap — for blind and visually impaired users and people with memory loss.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Blind and partially sighted people, their support organisations, care homes and dementia-care services in the EU.

The incumbent talking-label pen costs €100+ and uses proprietary labels; NFC readers, recorders and audio players are catalogue parts.

What you actually have to build

Tactile one-button pen, loud speaker, 1,000 notes on-board, washable/freezer-safe tag packs, optional phone app to type labels for text-to-speech.
