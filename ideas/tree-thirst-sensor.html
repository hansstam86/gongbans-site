Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Municipal green-space departments with climate-adaptation budgets (Vienna, Ludwigsburg, Jena and Pforzheim already run such programmes), tree-care contractors, housing associations with green space.

Cities already run funded tree-sensor programmes, the watering of young trees in dry summers is a line item every green department fights over, and the commercial sensors come with a platform fee the municipal IT would rather avoid.

What's new

What you actually have to build

A 60 cm stake with two capacitive moisture probes (30 and 60 cm), soil temperature, a sealed head with a Stamp C6LoRa and a lithium cell for five years, a soil-type calibration wizard, and open decoders for TTN and the city's LoRaWAN; a simple map and a watering list, no per-sensor fee.
