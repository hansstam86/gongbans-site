Matter Bridge for Old Air-Conditioners

A small IR puck that makes any infrared-remote split air-conditioner or air-to-air heat pump show up as a Matter thermostat, with a radar so it switches off when the room is empty.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Southern-European flats and the fast-growing base of air-to-air heat pumps in NL/Nordics; holiday rentals; small offices.

Air-to-air heat pumps are the cheapest heat-pump retrofit and sell in the millions, but most units only have an IR remote. Matter 1.2+ supports thermostats and room-air-conditioner device types in all major hubs.

What's new

What you actually have to build

IR code library for the top EU brands (learned + open databases), closes the loop on real room temperature instead of the unit's own sensor, empty-room setback after N minutes. Local Matter, no vendor cloud.

Certification path

RED (Wi-Fi/BLE module + 24 GHz radar), EMC, EN 62368-1, EN 18031 / CRA. No mains inside (USB-C).

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

Can we reliably decode/encode the IR protocols of the 15 best-selling split units in EU retail? If fewer than 12 work out of the box, the support cost kills the margin.
