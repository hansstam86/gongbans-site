Hay Spear

A one-metre temperature spear with LoRaWAN that lives inside the hay or straw stack and warns the farmer before self-heating turns into a barn fire.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Livestock and arable farms with hay or straw storage in DE/AT/CH/NL/FR; agricultural insurers as channel.

Hay self-ignition fires destroy barns every summer; insurers ask for monitoring; manual probing stops after a week. LoRaWAN reaches across farmyards and a 5-year battery node costs little.
