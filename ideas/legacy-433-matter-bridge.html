433 MHz Legacy-to-Matter Bridge

Plug-in bridge that listens to and replays the 433 MHz signals of old remote sockets, doorbells, awnings and window sensors so the house's existing cheap radio gadgets appear in Matter — no rip-and-replace.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Households with drawers of 433 MHz remote sockets and sensors (very common in DE/NL DIY stores); home-automation hobbyists.

Tens of millions of fixed-code 433 MHz devices are installed in Europe; Matter's bridge device type lets one box expose them as native accessories in every hub.

What's new

What you actually have to build

Learn mode decodes common fixed-code OOK protocols; Matter Bridge exposes each learned device as on/off, contact or button endpoint; local only.

Europe

Certification path

RED (433.05–434.79 MHz SRD + Wi-Fi), EMC, CRA. Must refuse rolling-code (garage door, alarm system) signals by design — no code-grabbing features.

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

What share of 433 MHz gear in a random sample of 30 homes is fixed-code and decodable? If under 60%, the 'works with what you have' promise fails.
