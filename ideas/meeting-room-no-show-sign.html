Meeting-Room No-Show Sign

An e-paper sign by the meeting-room door that shows the booking — and if nobody turns up within 10 minutes, radar notices the empty room and releases it automatically.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Offices with room-booking systems, co-working spaces, universities.

Ghost bookings are a top complaint about office space; room signs exist but don't know if people are actually inside. A presence radar inside plus e-paper outside fixes that.

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

Will an office with 20 rooms pay €229/room when cheaper non-sensing signs exist? Measure released hours in a 4-week pilot; need >15% more usable room time.
