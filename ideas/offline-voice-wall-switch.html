Offline Voice Wall Switch

A wall switch you can talk to — 'lights on', 'dim to half', 'kitchen off' — with all speech recognised on the device, no audio ever leaving the room, and Matter to talk to the rest of the house.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Privacy-minded households in DE/NL/AT who refuse cloud speakers; holiday-home owners; people whose hands are full (kitchens, workshops).

Offline keyword ASR modules are now a few euros; EU AI Act and GDPR debates make 'no cloud microphone' a selling point; Matter lets one switch drive any brand's lights.

What's new

What you actually have to build

Fixed vocabulary of ~30 commands per language, wake-word plus physical mute switch with hardware-cut LED; Matter client controls bound devices. Ships with DE/NL/EN command packs first (language support of the ASR chip must be verified).

Europe

Certification path

LVD (mains in wall box), RED via Thread module, EMC, CRA; GDPR: show that no audio is stored or transmitted (strong marketing claim, must be true in firmware audit).

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

Does the offline ASR reach >90% command accuracy in a real kitchen with a dishwasher running, in Dutch and German? Test 20 speakers before anything else.
