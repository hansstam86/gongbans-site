Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Independent retailers, bakeries, pharmacies, market stalls and small chains in the EU.

Electronic shelf labels are locked into enterprise systems with per-label fees; small shops still print paper daily; e-paper devices on ESP32 are cheap and run months on a charge.
