Bedroom Audit Box

A rental box that logs noise, light, CO2, temperature and humidity in a bedroom for seven nights and produces a plain-language sleep-environment report — rented out by mattress and sleep shops.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Mattress and bedding retailers, sleep-coaching services, wellness hotels; consumers via rental.

Sleep is a growth wellness category; sleep trackers measure the body, nobody measures the room; retailers want an advisory service that drives upsell.

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

Do 5 mattress shops rent out 3 boxes each for 2 months, and does the report change what customers buy?

Untested business model found in research (November 2026). The research found no precedent anywhere for the pack's core mechanic: a mattress or sleep retailer renting out a monitoring box and handing the customer a report. Every real competitor sells a tracker outright or uses sleep data only as an in-store sales aid. Treat the rental-through-retailer channel as the first thing to validate, ahead of the product; the kill question's five mattress shops are the right test, run it before anything else. As researched November 2026 by web search; verify before relying on it.
