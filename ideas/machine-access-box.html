Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Makerspaces and open workshops (Verbund Offener Werkstätten, FabLabs), school and university workshops, repair cafés; the space's board or the workshop manager pays.

FabAccess is being rolled out across ten workshops with public funding, which proves the sector wants machine access control; the commercial option bills per machine, and the catalogue has readers, relays and cores ready.

What's new

What you actually have to build

A DIN or wall box per machine with a reader, a relay that enables the machine's control circuit, an LED ring and buzzer, a local server (or FabAccess compatibility) holding members and training levels, usage logs and maintenance counters; no per-machine fee.

Certification path

RED (WiFi, 13.56 MHz reader) via modules, EMC, EN 62368-1 (low-voltage control circuit only), RoHS, WEEE, CRA; not a safety-rated interlock: it enables the control circuit, it does not replace the machine's e-stop.
