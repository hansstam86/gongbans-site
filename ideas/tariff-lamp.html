Tariff Lamp

A small lamp that glows green, amber or red with your actual hourly electricity price (and solar surplus) so the household runs the dishwasher when power is cheap — no app, no dashboard.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Dutch and German households on dynamic electricity contracts; home-automation enthusiasts; energy retailers as a branded giveaway.

Dynamic tariffs are now mass-market in NL and mandatory to offer in DE; dynamic grid fees arrive in 2026. Behaviour change needs a glanceable object, and no physical price-indicator lamp was found on the market.

What's new

What you actually have to build

90% firmware, 10% diffuser: pulls day-ahead prices from public market APIs, optional solar-surplus mode, local MQTT mode, captive-portal setup, signed OTA. One PCB spin: ESP32 module + 8 RGB LEDs + USB-C.
