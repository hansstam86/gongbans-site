Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Small online shops shooting product video, content creators, photo studios, schools' media labs in the EU.

Product video is now expected in e-commerce; motion-control rigs cost €1,000+; smart servo motors with RS485/CAN and a knob-with-screen exist in the catalogue.
