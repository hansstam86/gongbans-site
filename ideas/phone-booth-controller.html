Phone-Booth Fan & Light Controller

A controller for office phone booths and acoustic pods that keeps the fan and light on while someone sits still on a call, ramps ventilation by CO2, and reports usage — PIR timers in pods switch off on quiet people.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Acoustic-pod and office-furniture makers (OEM), facility managers retrofitting existing booths.

Pods are everywhere in hybrid offices and stuffy pods are the main complaint; CO2 sensors and still-person radar fix it for a few euros of parts.

Certification path

LVD + EMC (mains-fed), RED (radar), CRA if connected. Furniture makers will want EN 62368-1 test reports.

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

Will 2 pod manufacturers do a design-in for next year's model? OEM cycles are slow — get a letter of intent before tooling.
