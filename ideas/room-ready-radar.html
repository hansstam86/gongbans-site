Room-Ready Radar for Housekeeping

A small ceiling sensor per hotel room that tells housekeeping 'guest is out' right now — so staff stop knocking on occupied rooms — and the energy system can set back heating. Radar, no camera, no microphone.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Independent and mid-size hotels, aparthotels, student housing in EU cities.

Staff shortages make wasted housekeeping trips expensive; key-card pockets are bypassed; GDPR rules out cameras in rooms. Multi-target status radar modules are specifically aimed at hotels.

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

Will one 60-room hotel run a 20-room pilot and report fewer than 2 wrong 'vacant' calls per week? Hotel managers only buy after a pilot in their own building.
