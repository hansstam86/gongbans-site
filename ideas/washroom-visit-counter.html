Washroom Visit Counter

A radar at the washroom entrance that counts visits and triggers cleaning after N uses rather than on a fixed schedule — for offices, stations, festivals and sports venues, with no camera in or near the toilets.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Facility-service and cleaning companies, offices, event organisers, sports stadiums.

Cleaning contracts are shifting from fixed rounds to usage-based; toilets are the one place no camera can go. LoRaWAN coverage in commercial buildings is now normal.
