Isolated Field-Tech Interface Kit

A rugged pocket set of galvanically isolated USB-to-RS485/RS232/TTL adapters and a 4-port RS485 fan-out, so service technicians can talk to heat pumps, inverters and PLCs without frying a laptop.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

HVAC, solar and building-automation service technicians; industrial maintenance teams; distributors of installer tools.

Every heat pump and inverter now exposes Modbus; technicians use €5 unisolated dongles and lose laptops. Boring, low-risk, no radio — sellable as a pilot within a month.

What's new

What you actually have to build

Metal housing, labelled screw terminals, a cable set for the common heat-pump and inverter connectors, and a bundled Modbus scanner app.
