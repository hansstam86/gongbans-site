Isolated Bench Power Module

A breadboard-friendly module that gives analog and audio projects isolated ±12 V, ±5 V and 3.3 V from USB-C, plus an isolated USB-UART — the safe way to probe mains-adjacent or ground-loop-prone circuits.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Makers, electronics students, audio DIY community, repair cafés, test engineers.

Hobbyists blow up laptops and scopes through ground loops; isolated DC-DC converters with dual outputs are now cheap modules, and an open isolated USB adapter design already exists.

What's new

What you actually have to build

Rail selector jumpers, per-rail current limit and LED, isolated UART, clear isolation-barrier silkscreen and a 'what isolation does and doesn't protect' card.
