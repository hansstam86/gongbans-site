Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Supermarkets, pharmacies, public offices, hospitals' facility teams, airports' cleaning contractors in the EU.

The incumbent feedback terminals cost €100+ per month; public bodies are required to measure service satisfaction and dislike subscriptions; four buttons and a radio are trivial hardware.
