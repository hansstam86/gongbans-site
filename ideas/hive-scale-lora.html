Beehive Scale (sub-GHz)

A flat scale under a beehive that reports weight, temperature and air pressure over LoRa so beekeepers see nectar flow, swarming and winter-food levels without opening the hive.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Hobby and professional beekeepers (very large hobby base in DE, AT, CZ, SI, FR), beekeeping associations, agri-research.

Weight is the single most useful hive metric; existing hive scales are pricey and often need GSM. LoRa plus a public/association gateway makes a low-cost apiary network.
