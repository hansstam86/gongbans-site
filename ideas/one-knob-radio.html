One-Knob Radio

A beautiful wooden radio with a single big knob (on/volume) and preset stations or carer-loaded playlists, designed for people with dementia and for anyone who hates menus.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Families and care homes in DE/NL/UK/Nordics; dementia-care retailers.

Dementia prevalence rises with the age structure; existing simple players cost €100–250 and look clinical. The catalogue provides the sub-€5 speaker electronics.

What's new

What you actually have to build

Industrial design (wood, one knob, no visible buttons), carer-side setup via SD card or app, fail-safe 'it always plays something'.

Europe

Certification path

RED if Bluetooth/WiFi on (or ship a no-radio SKU: LVD/EMC only), EN 62368-1, RoHS/WEEE. No medical claims.

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

Five care-home activity coordinators try a 3D-printed prototype for 2 weeks: do residents use it unprompted, and would they buy 10 at €139?
