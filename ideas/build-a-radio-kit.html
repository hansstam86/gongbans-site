Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Secondary-school physics teachers, makerspaces, museum workshops and team-event organisers in DE/NL/UK.

Hands-on electronics kits are in demand for STEM curricula; a real analogue radio that works at the end of the afternoon is the classic payoff, and the open design needs no firmware.

What you actually have to build

Through-hole re-layout for beginners, numbered build sheet in four languages, wooden case, teacher's guide mapping to curriculum topics (waves, modulation).
