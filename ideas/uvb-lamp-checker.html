Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Reptile, tortoise and parrot keepers, pet shops and zoos in DE/UK/NL (Germany has the largest reptile-keeping community in Europe).

UVB meters are either €200+ specialist instruments or unreliable; keepers replace lamps by calendar; pet shops want a counter tool to sell lamps on evidence.
