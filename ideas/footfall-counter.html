Footfall Counter (GDPR by design)

A ceiling-mounted edge-AI counter that outputs only in/out numbers — never an image — at a third of the incumbents' price, for libraries, museums and independent shops.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Municipal libraries and museums, independent retailers, co-working spaces across the EU.

Public funding often requires visitor counts; incumbent stereo-vision counters cost €600–1,000+. The EU AI Act and data-protection scrutiny make 'no image ever leaves the device, auditable firmware' a real differentiator for public buyers.

What's new

What you actually have to build

Top-down counting model tuned for 2.5–4 m ceilings, dome enclosure, open REST/MQTT API and a one-page DPIA template the data officer can sign.

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

Run the K230 dev board + a webcam at 3 m above a friendly library entrance for a week against a manual tally. <92% after two tuning passes = stop.
