Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Secondary-school physics departments and vocational schools in DE/NL/UK/Nordics; educational distributors.

Schools replace analogue scopes with cheap digital ones that students cannot operate; curriculum reforms push measurement and data literacy; open handheld-scope designs exist.

The gongbans underneath

Boards from the catalogue and the role each plays. OPEN marks boards that publish schematic/PCB files; SPEC marks off-the-shelf modules or dev kits with documentation only. 4 of 4 boards publish schematic/PCB files.

What's new

What you actually have to build

Protected inputs (±30 V, no mains), experiment mode with guided measurements, USB export to the class spreadsheet, rugged case; class-set charging box.
