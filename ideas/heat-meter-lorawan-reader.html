Isolated Heat-Meter Reader (LoRaWAN)

A small isolated box that reads heat, cooling and water meters with an RS-485/Modbus output in apartment blocks and sends the readings over LoRaWAN — making old meters remotely readable without replacing them.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Housing associations, district-heating operators, metering service companies in NL, DE, DK, PL.

The EU Energy Efficiency Directive requires remotely readable heat meters and frequent consumption information to tenants; replacing working meters is expensive. Galvanic isolation avoids ground-loop failures in plant rooms.

What's new

What you actually have to build

Register maps for the most common Modbus-output meters, hourly/daily logs with local buffer, LoRaWAN payload codec, DIN-rail case. M-Bus would be a second variant.

Europe

Certification path

RED (868 MHz), EMC (industrial), EN 62368-1, CRA. Not a legal-metrology device: it transports readings, the meter stays the legal instrument.

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

Are enough installed heat meters Modbus-RS485 rather than wired M-Bus? Ask 3 housing associations for their meter inventory before building; if <30% Modbus, pivot to M-Bus first.
