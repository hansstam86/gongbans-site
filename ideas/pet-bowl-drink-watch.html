Pet Bowl Drink Watch

A weighing mat under the water bowl that learns how much your cat or dog normally drinks and tells you when that changes a lot — plus a 'bowl empty' alert when you're at work.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Cat and dog owners in DE/NL/UK/Nordics; veterinary clinics recommending it to owners of older pets.

Changes in drinking are something owners are often told to watch for in older pets; nobody measures it. A load cell and a cheap Wi-Fi module with a ready white-label app make it a fast product.
