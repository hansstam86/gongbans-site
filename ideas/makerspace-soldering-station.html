Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Makerspaces, FabLabs, vocational schools and university labs in the EU; electronics-education distributors.

Labs buy either €40 no-name stations that die or €400 branded ones; open controller designs with cartridge-tip support exist but lack a European safety file and spare-parts supply.

The gongbans underneath

Boards from the catalogue and the role each plays. OPEN marks boards that publish schematic/PCB files; SPEC marks off-the-shelf modules or dev kits with documentation only. 4 of 4 boards publish schematic/PCB files.

What's new

What you actually have to build

All-DC architecture with one certified external PSU (keeps mains out of the box), ESD-safe metal housing, spare tips and heaters stocked in the EU, classroom lock-out mode.
