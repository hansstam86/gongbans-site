ROS Trainer Robot

A ready-built ROS navigation robot on an open RK3566 board — lidar-ready, camera-ready — sold as a classroom platform for vocational schools and applied universities teaching mobile robotics.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Vocational schools, applied universities, robotics bootcamps and corporate training in DE/NL/FR/Nordics.

Industry demands ROS skills; existing education robots cost €3,000+; an open RK3566 ROS trolley design and open SBC exist.

Built from · 4 catalogue boards

The gongbans underneath

Boards from the catalogue and the role each plays. OPEN marks boards that publish schematic/PCB files; SPEC marks off-the-shelf modules or dev kits with documentation only. 3 of 4 boards publish schematic/PCB files; the rest are off-the-shelf modules or dev kits with documentation only.

What's new

What you actually have to build

English documentation and ROS 2 image, curriculum (SLAM, navigation, perception), swappable sensor deck, charging dock, EU spares.
