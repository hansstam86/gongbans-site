Matter Presence Puck

A coin-sized ceiling puck that knows someone is still in the room (reading, on the sofa) — unlike PIR — and reports occupancy over Matter-over-Thread to any EU smart-home hub, fully local, no app account.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Home Assistant / Apple Home / Google Home households in NL, DE, BE, Nordics; electricians fitting lighting automation in new builds.

Matter 1.3+ occupancy sensors are supported by all big hubs, Thread border routers now ship inside common speakers and TVs, and low-power 24 GHz presence radar finally makes a battery or USB puck practical. Lights that switch off while you sit still is the #1 complaint about PIR.

What's new

What you actually have to build

Firmware maps radar 'moving/static/none' to the Matter OccupancySensing cluster with tunable hold time; sensitivity zones set from the hub; one 30 mm PCB, USB-C or 2×AA. No cloud at all.

Certification path

RED for the 24 GHz radar module (short-range device band; confirm which harmonised standard the module's own test report covers); Thread radio via the module; Matter certification (CSA membership + test lab) is the real cost; EN 18031 / CRA as a connected product.

Before you cut a PCB

Kill question

Answer it for under €500

Will 200 Home Assistant users put down a €10 deposit for a €49 Matter radar puck when DIY ESPHome radar sensors already cost €15? Pass = 200 deposits in 6 weeks.
