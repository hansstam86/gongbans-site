Trail Talker

A solar-powered outdoor audio post that plays a local story when a visitor approaches — for nature trails, heritage sites and sculpture parks, with no app and no network.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Municipal parks departments, heritage trusts, nature reserves, open-air museums across Europe.

App-based audio guides have single-digit uptake; signage budgets exist; presence sensing and MP3 playback are trivial on the catalogue's boards.

What's new

What you actually have to build

IP66 post housing, multilingual button, content loaded by SD or BLE from a caretaker phone, winter power budget.
