Sip Coaster

A weighing coaster that knows how much you actually drank from the glass or bottle on it and glows gently when it has been too long since the last sip — for care homes, offices and anyone who forgets to drink.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Care homes and home-care services (hydration logging is a care KPI), offices, wellness retailers.

Care homes document fluid intake by hand; smart bottles exist but residents use their own cups; load cells and RGB light are cheap.

Certification path

RED + EN 18031, EMC, EN 62368-1, Li-ion UN 38.3, CRA, GDPR if intake is logged per resident. Wellness reminder only — no medical claims.
