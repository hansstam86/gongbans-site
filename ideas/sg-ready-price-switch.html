SG-Ready Price Switch

A DIN-rail box that closes a heat pump's SG-Ready or boiler contact — and up to four relays — when the hourly electricity price or your solar surplus says so, with no cloud account.

Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Homeowners with heat pumps or electric boilers on dynamic tariffs in NL/DE/AT; electricians who install heat pumps.

Most heat pumps have the SG-Ready contacts but nothing drives them; dynamic tariffs and variable grid fees make hour-shifting worth hundreds of euros a year.

What you actually have to build

Price fetch from public day-ahead markets, surplus logic from an optional energy-meter reader, schedule guard-rails (minimum run times), local web UI; sister product of the Tariff Lamp.
