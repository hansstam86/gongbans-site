Who buys · why now

The buyer in Europe

Battery repair shops, repair cafés, solar-storage DIY builders, vocational schools.

Right-to-repair rules and the Battery Regulation make pack rebuilding a legitimate trade; hobby welders from marketplaces have no documentation or CE file.

What's new

What you actually have to build

Interlocked enclosure, calibrated pulse tables for common nickel strip thicknesses, weld counter and QA log, EU-stocked electrodes.
