50 product ideas built from gongbans

Concepts a small European team could build mainly from boards that already exist in the catalogue: 50 ideas across 16 categories, referencing 143 real boards. Each one names the boards and their role, what is genuinely new, rough effort, BOM and price estimates, the EU certification path and the one cheap question that would kill it. Generated by the GONGBANS inventor from the public catalogue — concepts, not products.

How to read it: every card is one concept, scored 1–5 on Demand, Buildability from the catalogue, Margin and Speed to market (max 20). Click a card for the boards it uses, the estimates and the kill question. 25 ideas depend on community (OSHWHub) boards whose licences vary and may be non-commercial — they carry the tag check-licence. Board facts come from the catalogue; everything else is an estimate.

Founder start packs

Ideas marked Start pack come with a founder start pack: a one-page summary, a business plan (customer, market size, competition, unit economics, go-to-market, 12-month plan, funding, risks) and a start kit (the catalogue boards to copy from, a block diagram, a starter BOM, firmware starting points, a 12-week build plan, the EU certification checklist, a validation toolkit and a first-production checklist), ending with three things to do this week. Every pack is downloadable as Markdown. Same rule as the ideas: concepts, not products; every number is an estimate to check. Example: Tariff Lamp →
