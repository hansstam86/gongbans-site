Founder start packs

Ideas marked Start pack come with a founder start pack: a one-page summary, a business plan (customer, market size, competition, unit economics, go-to-market, 12-month plan, funding, risks) and a start kit (the catalogue boards to copy from, a block diagram, a starter BOM, firmware starting points, a 12-week build plan, the EU certification checklist, a validation toolkit and a first-production checklist), ending with three things to do this week. Every pack opens with Validate demand first: the idea's kill question as a go/no-go gate, where to find that specific audience, a problem-first interview script and a pre-order message, to be done before any sourcing (why and how →). Every pack also has a Who to contact section: the companies on file in gongban.db that publish the boards, the PCBA houses, and ready-to-send outreach e-mails in English and Chinese. Every pack is downloadable as Markdown. Same rule as the ideas: concepts, not products; every number is an estimate to check. Never built hardware? Read the zero-experience founder playbook first.
